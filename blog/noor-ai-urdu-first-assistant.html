Noor AI: Urdu-First Assistant Kaise Sochta Hai

Problem

Pakistan ke marketplaces me log aise likhte hain:

"sasta laal joota under 2k" · "bacha ke liye warm jacket 6 saal" · "khushbu jo 500 se kam ho"

Keyword search yahan fail ho jata hai, kyunki niyat alfaaz me nahi, sentence me hoti hai.

Noor ke teen asool

#AssoolMatlab
1Niyat pehle, alfaaz baad meSentence ko intent me parse karo — budget, rang, audience, category
2Urdu-first, English bhiRoman Urdu aur English dono samjho, jawab user ki zubaan me
3Imaandar jawabAgar exact match nahi hai to "sasta alternative ye hai" — jhooti listing nahi

Example pipeline

"Bhai 1500 me school bag chahiye" 
   → intent: { category: bag, audience: school, budget: 1500 }
   → filter: price <= 1500, stock > 0
   → rank: rating, delivery speed, COD
   → jawab: 3 options + ek honest note

Kya nahi karte

  • Fake reviews generate nahi karte
  • Clickbait price nahi dikhate (jo cart me badal jaye)
  • User ka data kisi third party ko nahi bechte
Noor ka matlab hi "roshni" hai — andhera nahi bechna.
