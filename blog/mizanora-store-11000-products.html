Mizanora Store: 11,000+ Products Kaise Manage Hote Hain

Shuruaat: 40 products se 11,000 tak

Mizanora store 2025 me chhoti si list se shuru hua. Aaj 11,000+ products hain — aur sab kuch ek insaan ne
saste phone par test karke manage kiya. Numbers impress karte hain, lekin asli sawal ye hai:
catalog ko saaf kaise rakha jaye?

Teen masle jo har catalog me aate hain

MaslaKya hota haiSolution
DuplicateEk hi product 4 naam seSlug + image-hash dedupe
Dead stock800 products jinme stock 0Auto-hide + "coming back" tag
Jhooti priceListing 999, cart me 1299Price = final price, koi surprise nahi

Noor AI ka role

Noor sirf chatbot nahi — wo catalog ka quality gate hai:

  • Product title se category suggest karta hai
  • Missing size/color attributes pakadta hai
  • Duplicate detection me help karta hai (same image, same specs)

Urdu-first search kaise chalta hai

Customer likhta hai: "sasta laal joota under 2k".

intent = { category: "joota", color: "laal", budget: 2000 }
→ filter → rank (rating, COD, delivery speed) → 3 honest options

Agar exact match nahi hai to Noor saaf keh deta hai — jhooti listing nahi dikhata.

Worker / reseller programme

Har worker ko apna link, apna wallet aur apna dashboard milta hai. Commission transparent hai,
payout record ledger me hai — koi "bhai baad me dekh lenge" nahi.

Sabaq

Catalog ek product nahi, system hai. System saaf ho to 11,000 products bhi sambhal jate hain,
system ganda ho to 100 products bhi afra-tafra hote hain.
